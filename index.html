Try the flow

  1. Open a queue, for example the visa center.
  2. Buy a spot from the list to jump ahead, or
  3. Join the line to get your own ticket with a QR code.
  4. On your ticket, set a price and list it. A mock buyer appears after a few seconds.
  5. Check Wallet to see the money move.

User-experience prototype for the student hackathon. Venues, wait times, tickets, prices and transactions are mock data. No wallet connects and no money moves.
